The programs, in detail

Four programs of 6 to 16 weeks, timed to your growing season and your local frost dates.

Program 01 · From lawn to harvest

Your first vegetable bed

New gardeners plant too much, too close, and water too little but too often. We start with one bed sized to what you'll maintain, choose crops suited to your climate and your patience, and set a watering routine that goes deep twice a week instead of a daily sprinkle.

  • 16 weeks
  • Complete beginners
  • 3 hours a week
  • A sunny patch or containers

Program 02 · Where yields come from

Soil & feeding

Most disappointing harvests are soil problems wearing a disguise. We start with a proper test — usually through your county extension service, which is cheap and accurate — then fix structure with compost and mulch, and feed only where a test says it's needed.

  • 10 weeks
  • Any level
  • 2 hours a week
  • A soil test

Start this program

Program 03 · Something to pick every week

Season planning

A well-planned small garden beats a large chaotic one. We build a calendar from your frost dates, stagger sowings so harvests spread out, and plan rotation so the same family isn't in the same bed every year.

  • 8 weeks
  • One season in
  • 2 hours a week
  • Your frost dates

Program 04 · Diagnose before you spray

Pests & problems

Yellow leaves have a dozen causes and most sprays treat none of them. We diagnose from photographs, work through the likely causes, and start with the least drastic response. Anything you do apply must follow its label and your local rules — we'll say so every time.

  • 6 weeks
  • Any level
  • 2 hours a week
  • A camera phone
