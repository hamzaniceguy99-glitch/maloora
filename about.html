How we work

Your conditions, your calendar

Gardening content is written for an average garden that doesn't exist. Frost dates, humidity, soil type and how many hours of direct sun your yard gets change every recommendation.

So everything here starts with your specifics: a soil test, sun hours measured rather than guessed, and a sowing calendar built on your local dates. It's less exciting than a list of ten easy crops and it produces food.

The other half is diagnosis. A photo of a leaf, top and bottom, usually answers what three hours of searching won't.

Soil first
