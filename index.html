How we work

A coach, not a garden center

Garden advice usually ends at a product: a fertilizer, a spray, a raised-bed kit. We sell nothing, so the answer can be compost, better spacing, or simply watering less often.

Everything is tied to your actual conditions — your frost dates, your sun hours, your soil test. That's the difference between advice that works and advice written for a different state.

Frequently asked

The questions we get most

I have no yard, just a balcony.

Containers work well for a surprising amount. We'll plan for the light you have and the pot sizes that make sense.

How do I test my soil?

Your county extension service usually does it cheaply and reliably. We'll help you read the results, which is the part people find confusing.

Can you tell me what's wrong with my plant?

Usually, from good photographs — leaves top and bottom, the whole plant, and the soil. Some things need a lab, and we'll say when.

Do you recommend sprays?

Rarely, and only after diagnosis. Anything you use must follow its label and your local regulations, which we'll point you to rather than improvising.

I killed everything last year.

The most common starting point. It's nearly always watering, sun hours or spacing — all fixable before the next season.

Can I cancel anytime?

Yes. No contract, no minimum term. Access stays open until the end of the month you already paid for.

What do you want to grow?

Twenty minutes on your space, your climate and what's realistic this season. Free.
